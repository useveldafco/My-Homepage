I am an LLM researcher affiliated with the University. My work focuses on large language model architectures, efficient training and inference, ethical LLM deployment, and adaptive learning systems.

## Research

My research aims to make language models more capable, efficient, and responsible. My paper ["Ipsum Lorem is all you need"](https://openreview.net/forum?id=example123) was selected for an oral presentation at COML 2025, placing it among the top approximately 1% of submissions.

Selected research directions include:

- Efficient LLM architectures and reduced computational cost
- Contextual reasoning and optimization of language models
- Ethical deployment and responsible use of LLMs
- Adaptive learning systems that incorporate model feedback
